<h2> About us</h2>
<p>The Data Club of Notre Dame is a student-led organization that partners with companies and organizations each semester to work on data-focused projects. Our goal is to give students hands-on experience with real-world data through connections with our partners, access to nationwide competitions, and learning resources.</p>
